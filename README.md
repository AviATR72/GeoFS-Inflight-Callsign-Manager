# GeoFS-Inflight-Callsign-Manager
Lets you easily change, save and manage your GeoFS callsign in flight without having to keep going to your account page

GeoFS Inflight Callsign Manager
This addon lets you change your callsign whilst flying, instead of clicking onto your account screen and having to manually change it. Once activated the normal GeoFS account page button is used to change your callsign. Additionally, it prevents you from accidentally clicking your account button during a flight and losing all your flight progress!

During a flight, your callsign can be changed by clicking the normal account/callsign button in the top right. Instead of taking you to the account screen, a menu will be displayed that lets you change your callsign midair instead of being taken to the account page. A textbox lets you type your desired callsign while it stores any past callsigns in an easy menu.

Installation is done with the Dev console. To install the manager script, copy the code from the "Callsign Manager Script" file, go to GeoFS, click "FLY", and open the Console. Paste the code into the console and click "Enter".

As shown in this photo, there is still an option to be taken to the normal account screen at the bottom of the addon menu
